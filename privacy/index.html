Developer: Dark Carrot
Last updated: 13 October 2026

English version below · Türkçe sürüm için tıklayın

This policy applies to every mobile application published by Dark Carrot that links to this page. None of our applications ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

The sections below describe each type of processing. A section applies to an application only if the table above marks that feature for it. When we publish a new application, this table is updated before that application is released.

2. Advertising

Applications marked "Advertising" show advertisements through the advertising network named for them in the table above. Only the subsection for that network applies to an application.

2.1 Google AdMob

Applications that use Google AdMob show banner and interstitial advertisements. To serve and measure ads, Google collects and processes:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate, country-level location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system version and app version;
  • information about ads shown to you and your interactions with them.

This data is collected by Google as an independent controller. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choice: you can reset or delete your advertising identifier, and opt out of ad personalisation, from your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android devices). Doing so does not remove ads, but they will no longer be personalised.

2.2 Unity Ads and Unity LevelPlay

Applications that use Unity Ads show only optional rewarded video ads: in Brick Out, when you lose your last ball you may choose to watch a short video to get one more ball. No ad is shown unless you tap to watch one, and there are no banner or interstitial ads. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (Unity's ad mediation platform, formerly ironSource), with Unity Ads as the ad network; both are provided by Unity Technologies. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Unity collects and processes:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language, network type and app version;
  • information about ads shown to you and your interactions with them (for example, whether a video was watched to the end or tapped);
  • diagnostic data about how the advertising software is performing.

This data is collected by Unity as an independent controller. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, which also explains how to make a privacy request to Unity.

Your choice: the first time you open the application, it asks whether our ad partners may use your device data to show ads that suit you. The advertising software is not started until you answer. You can change your answer at any time under Settings → Personalized ads in the application. If you decline, rewarded ads remain available but are not personalised. You can also reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalisation from your device settings, as described in 2.1.

3. Leaderboards (Google Play Games Services)

Where an application offers leaderboards, signing in to Google Play Games is optional and the application can be used in full without it. If you choose to sign in, Google Play Games Services processes your Play Games player identifier and the score you submit, so that your score can be shown on the leaderboard. We never receive your Google account password and we do not receive your e-mail address.

You can review or delete your Play Games data at any time from the Google Play Games application (Settings → Delete Play Games account data).

4. Remote configuration

Applications marked "Remote configuration" download a small settings file when they start, from static hosting on GitHub Pages under https://darkcarrot-app.github.io/. The file contains only operational settings, such as how often an advertisement is shown. No personal data is sent in this request and we do not operate the server. As with any request to a web server, GitHub may process the requesting IP address and user agent in its own logs, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive, store or have access to those logs.

5. Data stored on your device only

Preferences such as sound and music settings, vibration, chosen language, your answer to the personalised-ads question, progress, scores and high scores are saved locally on your device using standard application storage. This information is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the application.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs of our own.
  • We do not offer in-app purchases and do not process any payment information.
  • We do not require registration and do not maintain user accounts.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade any information.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

8. Children

Our applications are intended for a general audience. They are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through one of our applications, please contact us at the address below and we will take appropriate action.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data: removed when you uninstall the application.
  • Leaderboard data: held by Google Play Games Services and removable through the Google Play Games app.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google or Unity, depending on the application (section 2), according to their own policies and retention periods.
  • Hosting logs: any logs created when the settings file is downloaded are held by GitHub under its own privacy statement; we neither receive nor retain them.

How to request deletion of your data

To request deletion of data relating to your use of a Dark Carrot application, follow the steps below. This applies to Color Up!, Brick Out and every other application listed in section 1.

  1. Data stored on your device — uninstall the application. Preferences, progress and scores saved on the device are deleted immediately by Android. Nothing is retained by us, because this data never leaves your device.
  2. Leaderboard data — open the Google Play Games application, go to Settings → Delete Play Games account data, and follow the instructions there. Google deletes your player profile and submitted scores. We cannot delete this data on your behalf because it is held by Google, not by us.
  3. Advertising data — open your device settings and go to Settings → Privacy → Ads, then delete or reset your advertising ID. Google's handling and deletion of advertising data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; Unity's, for applications that use Unity Ads, by the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, which explains how to send Unity a deletion request.
  4. Any other request — write to darkcarrotgames@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the name of the application. We reply within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is retained. We operate no user database and no server that stores user data, so there is nothing on our side to delete: we hold no account, no profile, no progress and no identifier belonging to you, and therefore we apply no additional retention period. The data described in sections 2 and 3 is held by Google and is deleted according to Google's own retention periods, using the steps above; for applications that use Unity Ads, the advertising data described in section 2.2 is held by Unity and deleted according to Unity's own retention periods. Hosting logs described in section 4 belong to GitHub and are retained under the GitHub Privacy Statement; we have no access to them.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data relating to you, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because we do not maintain user accounts, we generally hold no data that identifies you personally. Requests concerning advertising data are handled by Google or Unity, and requests concerning Play Games data by Google; please use the links above. For anything else, contact us and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

2. Reklamlar

"Reklam" işaretli uygulamalar, yukarıdaki tabloda kendileri için belirtilen reklam ağı üzerinden reklam gösterir. Bir uygulama için yalnızca o ağın alt bölümü geçerlidir.

2.1 Google AdMob

Google AdMob kullanan uygulamalar banner ve geçiş reklamları gösterir. Reklamların sunulması ve ölçülmesi için Google şu verileri toplar ve işler:

  • cihazınızın reklam kimliği (Android Reklam Kimliği);
  • IP adresiniz ve bundan türetilen ülke düzeyinde yaklaşık konum;
  • cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi sürümü ve uygulama sürümü gibi cihaz ve uygulama bilgileri;
  • size gösterilen reklamlar ve bu reklamlarla etkileşimleriniz hakkında bilgiler.

Bu veriler Google tarafından bağımsız veri sorumlusu sıfatıyla toplanır. Ayrıntılar için Google Gizlilik Politikası ve Google'ın hizmetlerini kullanan site ve uygulamalardan gelen bilgileri nasıl kullandığı sayfalarına bakabilirsiniz.

Seçeneğiniz: Reklam kimliğinizi cihaz ayarlarından sıfırlayabilir veya silebilir, reklam kişiselleştirmesini kapatabilirsiniz (çoğu Android cihazda Ayarlar → Gizlilik → Reklamlar). Bu, reklamları kaldırmaz; yalnızca kişiselleştirilmelerini engeller.

2.2 Unity Ads ve Unity LevelPlay

Unity Ads kullanan uygulamalar yalnızca isteğe bağlı ödüllü video reklamlar gösterir: Brick Out'ta son topunuzu kaybettiğinizde, bir top daha kazanmak için kısa bir video izlemeyi seçebilirsiniz. Siz izlemek için dokunmadıkça reklam gösterilmez; banner veya geçiş reklamı yoktur. Reklamlar, Unity'nin reklam aracılık platformu Unity LevelPlay (eski adıyla ironSource) üzerinden, reklam ağı olarak Unity Ads ile sunulur; ikisi de Unity Technologies tarafından sağlanır. Reklamların sunulması, ölçülmesi ve dolandırıcılığın önlenmesi için Unity şu verileri toplar ve işler:

  • cihazınızın reklam kimliği (Android Reklam Kimliği) ve diğer cihaz kimlikleri;
  • IP adresiniz ve bundan türetilen yaklaşık konum;
  • cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi sürümü, dil, ağ türü ve uygulama sürümü gibi cihaz ve uygulama bilgileri;
  • size gösterilen reklamlar ve bunlarla etkileşimleriniz hakkında bilgiler (örneğin bir videonun sonuna kadar izlenip izlenmediği veya videoya dokunulup dokunulmadığı);
  • reklam yazılımının nasıl çalıştığına dair tanılama verileri.

Bu veriler Unity tarafından bağımsız veri sorumlusu sıfatıyla toplanır. Ayrıntılar ve Unity'ye gizlilik talebinde bulunma yolu için Unity Oyuncu ve Uygulama Kullanıcısı Gizlilik Politikası'na bakabilirsiniz.

Seçeneğiniz: Uygulamayı ilk açtığınızda, reklam ortaklarımızın size uygun reklamlar göstermek için cihaz verilerinizi kullanıp kullanamayacağı sorulur. Reklam yazılımı siz yanıt verene kadar başlatılmaz. Yanıtınızı istediğiniz zaman uygulamadaki Ayarlar → Kişisel reklam seçeneğinden değiştirebilirsiniz. Reddederseniz ödüllü reklamlar yine kullanılabilir, ancak kişiselleştirilmez. Ayrıca 2.1'de anlatıldığı gibi reklam kimliğinizi cihaz ayarlarından sıfırlayabilir veya reklam kişiselleştirmesini kapatabilirsiniz.

Play Games verilerinizi dilediğiniz zaman Google Play Games uygulamasından inceleyebilir veya silebilirsiniz (Ayarlar → Play Games hesap verilerini sil).

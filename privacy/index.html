Last updated: 6 September 2026

This policy applies to every mobile application published by Dark Carrot that links to this page. None of our applications ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

The sections below describe each type of processing. A section applies to an application only if the table above marks that feature for it. When we publish a new application, this table is updated before that application is released.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Applications marked "Advertising" show banner and interstitial advertisements through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google collects and processes:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate, country-level location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system version and app version;
  • information about ads shown to you and your interactions with them.

This data is collected by Google as an independent controller. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choice: you can reset or delete your advertising identifier, and opt out of ad personalisation, from your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android devices). Doing so does not remove ads, but they will no longer be personalised.

3. Leaderboards (Google Play Games Services)

Where an application offers leaderboards, signing in to Google Play Games is optional and the application can be used in full without it. If you choose to sign in, Google Play Games Services processes your Play Games player identifier and the score you submit, so that your score can be shown on the leaderboard. We never receive your Google account password and we do not receive your e-mail address.

You can review or delete your Play Games data at any time from the Google Play Games application (Settings → Delete Play Games account data).

4. Remote configuration

Applications marked "Remote configuration" download a small settings file when they start, from static hosting on GitHub Pages under https://darkcarrot-app.github.io/. The file contains only operational settings, such as how often an advertisement is shown. No personal data is sent in this request and we do not operate the server. As with any request to a web server, GitHub may process the requesting IP address and user agent in its own logs, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive, store or have access to those logs.

5. Data stored on your device only

Preferences such as sound and music settings, chosen language, progress, scores and high scores are saved locally on your device using standard application storage. This information is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the application.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs of our own.
  • We do not offer in-app purchases and do not process any payment information.
  • We do not require registration and do not maintain user accounts.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade any information.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

8. Children

Our applications are intended for a general audience. They are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through one of our applications, please contact us at the address below and we will take appropriate action.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data: removed when you uninstall the application.
  • Leaderboard data: held by Google Play Games Services and removable through the Google Play Games app.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google according to its own policies and retention periods.
  • Hosting logs: any logs created when the settings file is downloaded are held by GitHub under its own privacy statement; we neither receive nor retain them.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data relating to you, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because we do not maintain user accounts, we generally hold no data that identifies you personally. Requests concerning advertising data or Play Games data are handled by Google; please use the links above. For anything else, contact us and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

If this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. The table in section 1 is kept current: a new application is added to it before that application is released.

2. Reklamlar (Google AdMob)

"Reklam" işaretli uygulamalar, Google AdMob aracılığıyla banner ve geçiş reklamları gösterir. Reklamların sunulması ve ölçülmesi için Google şu verileri toplar ve işler:

  • cihazınızın reklam kimliği (Android Reklam Kimliği);
  • IP adresiniz ve bundan türetilen ülke düzeyinde yaklaşık konum;
  • cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi sürümü ve uygulama sürümü gibi cihaz ve uygulama bilgileri;
  • size gösterilen reklamlar ve bu reklamlarla etkileşimleriniz hakkında bilgiler.

Bu veriler Google tarafından bağımsız veri sorumlusu sıfatıyla toplanır. Ayrıntılar için Google Gizlilik Politikası ve Google'ın hizmetlerini kullanan site ve uygulamalardan gelen bilgileri nasıl kullandığı sayfalarına bakabilirsiniz.

Seçeneğiniz: Reklam kimliğinizi cihaz ayarlarından sıfırlayabilir veya silebilir, reklam kişiselleştirmesini kapatabilirsiniz (çoğu Android cihazda Ayarlar → Gizlilik → Reklamlar). Bu, reklamları kaldırmaz; yalnızca kişiselleştirilmelerini engeller.

3. Skor tabloları (Google Play Games Services)

Bir uygulama skor tablosu sunuyorsa, Google Play Games'e giriş yapmak isteğe bağlıdır ve uygulama giriş yapılmadan da tam olarak kullanılabilir. Giriş yapmayı seçerseniz, Google Play Games Services skorunuzun tabloda gösterilebilmesi için Play Games oyuncu kimliğinizi ve gönderdiğiniz skoru işler. Google hesap şifrenizi hiçbir zaman görmüyoruz; e-posta adresiniz de bize iletilmiyor.

Play Games verilerinizi dilediğiniz zaman Google Play Games uygulamasından inceleyebilir veya silebilirsiniz (Ayarlar → Play Games hesap verilerini sil).

4. Uzaktan yapılandırma

"Uzaktan yapılandırma" işaretli uygulamalar açılışta, https://darkcarrot-app.github.io/ altında GitHub Pages üzerinde barındırılan küçük bir ayar dosyası indirir. Bu dosyada yalnızca reklamın hangi sıklıkta gösterileceği gibi işletimsel ayarlar bulunur. Bu istekte hiçbir kişisel veri gönderilmez ve sunucuyu biz işletmiyoruz. Herhangi bir web sunucusuna yapılan her istekte olduğu gibi GitHub, kendi kayıtlarında isteği yapan IP adresini ve kullanıcı aracısı bilgisini işleyebilir; ayrıntılar GitHub Gizlilik Beyanı'nda açıklanmıştır. Bu kayıtlar bize iletilmez, tarafımızca saklanmaz ve bunlara erişimimiz yoktur.

5. Yalnızca cihazınızda saklanan veriler

Ses ve müzik tercihi, seçilen dil, ilerleme, skorlar ve en yüksek skor gibi bilgiler standart uygulama deposu kullanılarak yalnızca cihazınızda saklanır. Bu bilgiler bize veya başka birine iletilmez ve uygulamayı kaldırdığınızda silinir.
